As memory-intensive applications, particularly language models, continue to grow, Processing-in-Memory (PIM) has emerged as a promising architecture to alleviate memory bandwidth limitations. We find that PIM systems need to support both spatio and temporal sharing between domains to extract the maximal performance. However, current PIM systems lack the necessary protection to securely support such sharing. To address these issues, we introduce *SecPIMlet*, a trusted multi-domain PIM that enables efficient spatio-temporal sharing. For fast and confidential spatio-temporal sharing, *SecPIMlet* protects the PIM-resident data and execution context and also protects in-transit data between the CPU and PIM. Our evaluation shows that SecPIMlet delivers 175.4% of throughput improvement compared to prior inefficient spatio-temporal sharing, while providing a robust security solution.
